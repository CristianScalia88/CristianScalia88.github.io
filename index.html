🫧 The Game

Every bubble is a step closer to a rescue

Watercolor islands, simple mechanics, and kittens with unique personalities. Designed for short, relaxing sessions — perfect for wait times or right before sleeping.

Aim and Match

Precise shots, combos of 3 or more bubbles, and hand-crafted levels — never overwhelming, always satisfying.

Progress at Your Pace

Clear objectives, three stars per level, and optional power-ups that are never required to progress.

A World to Breathe

Between levels, your rescued kittens rest in the island sanctuary. No rush, no guilt, just you and the tide.

🎯 Three Steps

How to Play

It's that simple to play your first game — the rest you learn by playing, with no interrupting tutorials.

1

Choose Your Island

Each archipelago has its own biome, watercolor palette, and a kitten waiting to be rescued at the end.

2

Aim and Shoot

Match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them. Clear the board before you run out of moves.

3

Rescue and Collect

Each completed island unlocks a new feline companion, featuring unique abilities to help you in your next game.
